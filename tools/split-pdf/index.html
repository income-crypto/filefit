How to extract pages from a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF file you want to work with.
  2. Enter page numbers or ranges separated by commas, for example 1-3, 6, 9-11.
  3. Click Extract pages and download the new PDF. Confirm the selected pages are present.

What this PDF splitter does

This tool extracts the selected pages into one output PDF. It does not automatically create a separate file for every individual page. Page numbers start at 1, and the extracted pages are saved in their original document order.

For example, entering 2-4, 7 keeps pages 2, 3, 4 and 7. An invalid range or an encrypted PDF may cause an error. Check the page count in your original document before choosing a range.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Split PDF FAQs

Can I extract page 2 and page 5 only?

Yes. Enter 2, 5 in the page-range field and download the resulting PDF.

Will I get one PDF per page?

No. Selected pages are combined into one new PDF file.

Does splitting delete the original pages?

No. Your original PDF remains unchanged on your device.
