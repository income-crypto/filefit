How to make a PDF from images

How do I turn multiple photos into one PDF? Select up to 20 JPG, PNG or WebP images, put them in the desired order, and create one PDF with one image per page. You can choose A4, landscape, Letter or original-fit sizing. This tool does not perform OCR, so text in pictures does not become selectable.

  1. Choose one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images.
  2. Arrange the images in the order you want and select A4, landscape, letter or original-fit page sizing.
  3. Click Create PDF and download the result. Review all pages before sending the document.

Keep your pages readable

One image becomes one PDF page. For multi-page assignments and forms, check that all pages are upright, clear and in the correct order. A4 portrait is a common choice for documents, while landscape can be more suitable for wider images.

Filefit generates the document inside the browser. It is not an OCR tool: text inside an image remains part of the image rather than becoming selectable text. Very large photo collections may require substantial device memory.

For multi-page forms and assignments, follow the step-by-step image-to-PDF checklist to verify order, orientation, margins and readability.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Images to PDF FAQs

Can I combine several images into one PDF?

Yes. Select multiple supported images and arrange them before processing.

Does the PDF contain selectable text?

No. Image-to-PDF conversion embeds the image visually; it does not perform optical character recognition.

Can I choose A4 size?

Yes. A4 portrait, landscape, US Letter and original-fit options are available.
