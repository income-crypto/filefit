GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is Filefit really free?

Yes. The six tools are free to use without an account, subscription, or watermark.

Does Filefit upload my files to a server?

No. Files are processed locally by your browser. PDF functions load an open-source JavaScript library hosted on Filefit. File contents remain in your browser.

Can I make a photo exactly 20 KB?

You can set a target of 20 KB or a custom limit. Image compression is best-effort: the result depends on the image's detail and format. We'll tell you the actual final size.

Why won't my password-protected PDF work?

Encrypted and some malformed PDFs may not be supported. Unlock the file in a trusted PDF editor first and ensure you have permission to modify it.
